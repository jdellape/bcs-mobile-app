Blair County Services

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Blair County Services is a public directory of social service agencies in Blair County, Pennsylvania. This policy describes how the iPhone app handles information.

Information the app does not collect

The public App Store version does not create accounts, does not require sign-in, and does not ask you for your name, email, phone number, or location. It does not use advertising or analytics SDKs.

Information the app displays

The app fetches publicly maintained agency records from our backend (name, address, phone number, and a description of services) and shows them so you can find help. That directory information belongs to the listed agencies, not to you as a user.

Technical communication

When you search, the app makes HTTPS requests to our API. Standard server logs (such as time, IP address, and the requested URL) may be created by the hosting provider as part of operating the service. We do not use those logs to build a profile of you.

Children

The app is a general-audience directory and is not directed at children under 13.
